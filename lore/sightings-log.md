# Sightings Log

Recorded encounters with [[seven|Seven]]. Maintained by the Field Service across all field offices. Entries are accepted from agents, provisional inductees, and — increasingly — civilians who have reported encounters convincing enough that an agent forwarded them to the log.

The log is not a directory of who Seven is. It is a record of what Seven *did*, where, and to whom. Theories about Seven's identity ([[who-is-seven]]) are not for the log.

## Format

Each entry contains:

- **Date** (as accurate as the source allows; "circa" is fine; "during the spring rains" is fine)
- **Location** (where the encounter took place, in terms native to the time and place)
- **Reporter** (agent designation, or "civilian, name withheld," or "anonymous")
- **The encounter** (what happened, in the reporter's own words where possible)
- **What was handed off** (the question asked, the object given, the sentence spoken, the song hummed, the direction pointed)
- **Signature** (the seven-dot Pleiades mark, if Seven left one)

## Sample entries (working set)

To be populated. Goal is a few dozen across many eras. A representative sketch of where entries should range:

- A tablet fragment from Ur, ~2400 BCE. Reporter unknown. Encounter described in third person.
- A monastery scribe's marginalia, ~9th century, somewhere on the Silk Road.
- A field agent's report, 1923, Cairo bazaar. Object handed off: a single dried apricot.
- A letter, 1969, Bethel NY. Reporter: a fairgoer. Question asked: "what are you listening for?"
- A bus diary, 1974. Reporter: a touring guitarist. Hand-off: a song they could never quite reproduce afterward.
- A text message thread, 2008, a Brooklyn loft party. Hand-off: the direction the stranger pointed before leaving.
- A voice memo, last spring, festival grounds in the western US. Hand-off: a pressed flower.

## Entries from Seven Stars festival

To be added during and after the weekend. Any agent on shift can record an encounter. Any provisional inductee can report one. Civilians whose accounts pass an agent's gut check get included.

## Editorial notes

- Don't sanitize entries. The reporter's language is part of the record.
- Don't try to reconcile contradictions. Two encounters that contradict each other are both true. That's the whole point.
- Don't speculate in the log itself. Speculation belongs in [[who-is-seven]].
- The seven-dot mark, when present, is reproduced as drawn — never the same twice.
