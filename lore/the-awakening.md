# The Awakening

For most of recorded history, Earth (Sol-3) was a quiet posting. The [[resonance-grid|Grid]] signal here was present but unremarkable. Readings were taken remotely. No in-person field office.

Then, sometime in the mid-20th century, the signal lit up.

## What woke it up

Two things, mostly, and they happened together: **the dance floor and the chemistry.**

Humans figured out — gradually, then suddenly — that they could gather in large numbers, play loud music, share psychoactive substances, and synchronize. Not metaphorically. Physically. Same beat, same breath, same room, sometimes the same chemical compound running through everyone's nervous system. The local Grid signal responded by becoming one of the brightest spikes in the sector.

That's the whole story. There is no hidden cause. No buried artifact. No alien intervention. Humans found their own dance floor and the Grid noticed.

## When the field office opened

Once the readings became too strong to ignore, an in-person field detachment was authorized. Earth went from a remote-monitoring posting to one of the most-requested gigs in the agency. It still is.

## Why Earth keeps spiking

Because they keep doing it. Every festival, every show, every wedding reception, every kitchen-floor dance party — the signal feeds. Earth has unusual cultural infrastructure for generating connection at scale. The Grid likes it. We like it.

## What this is NOT

- Not a conspiracy. Nobody woke Earth up. Earth woke itself up.
- Not a power-extraction story. The Grid isn't being mined. The Grid is being fed, and Earth eats well.
- Not over. The signal is still climbing.
