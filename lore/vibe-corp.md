# Vibe Corp

The outfit. The one we work for. The one we *are*, more accurately — there isn't really an "us and them" with Vibe Corp, because Vibe Corp is just everyone who's ever done the work, plus whatever's left of the ones who came before.

## What we do

Tend the [[resonance-grid]]. Take readings. Patch leaks. Witness the signal. Hand commendations to beings who are doing the work of connection well, whether or not they know that's what they're doing. Most of them don't.

## How we came to be

Not founded. **Accreted.**

Somewhere a very long time ago, a being heard the Grid for the first time and started listening on purpose. They told a friend. The friend told a friend. The chain of listeners grew, picked up habits, eventually printed badges. Vibe Corp is what builds up around something true if you give it enough centuries.

There is no founder. There is no charter. Nobody can tell you what kind of thing Vibe Corp actually is — a company, a guild, a lineage, a long-running misunderstanding — and the ones who've looked into it come back with different answers. Old. Older than most things. Nobody knows how old.

The name is just what it's called. It has been called other things. It'll probably be called something else eventually.

## HQ

The home office. Where the badges come from, where in theory someone is in charge.

Whether anyone is *actually* at HQ running things, in any meaningful sense, is a question that's quietly nagged the field for a long time. The badges arrive. The pay clears. The walkie-talkie sometimes picks up wind. See [[empty-hq]].

## Tone

Vibe Corp takes itself seriously without being self-serious. The work is real. The Grid is real. We laugh on shift because the Grid likes laughter — it's not a break from the work, it's part of the work.
