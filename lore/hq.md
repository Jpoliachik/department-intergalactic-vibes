# HQ

The home office. Where the paperwork goes. Where the badges come from. Where, in theory, someone is in charge.

## What we know

- It's old. Older than the Department's own founding records, depending on who you ask. Don't ask.
- It runs on forms. There is a form for every reading, citation, commendation, and coffee break.
- Badges arrive in the mail. Paychecks clear. Memos get sent. Field reports get filed and, presumably, read.
- The walkie-talkie connects to HQ. Sometimes. See [[transmissions]].

## What we don't know

A lot.

No field agent currently on the books has visited HQ in person. The address on the forms is correct. Agents who've gone looking have either come back with descriptions that don't match each other, or come back saying it's nice but quiet, or not come back from the visit with much to say at all.

The Department's leadership structure on paper is elaborate. The Department's leadership structure in practice is — unclear. Memos get signed but the signatures are old and reused. Decisions get made but it's hard to trace where. The agency runs. The Grid gets tended. Whether anyone is actually steering is an open question.

See [[empty-hq]] for the thread.

## Office posture toward HQ

Affectionate. Slightly suspicious. Mostly we let HQ do its thing and we do ours. The forms are filed. The signal is tended. If HQ is on top of it, great. If HQ is a thousand-year-old empty building still mailing badges by momentum alone — also okay. The work is the work either way.
