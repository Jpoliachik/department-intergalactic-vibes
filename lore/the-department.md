# The Department of Intergalactic Vibrations

D.I.V. The agency. The one we work for. The one we *are*, more accurately — there isn't really an "us and them" with the Department, because the Department is just every agent who's ever served, plus a lot of filing cabinets.

## What we do

Tend the [[resonance-grid]]. Take readings. Patch leaks. Witness the signal. Issue commendations to beings who are doing the work of connection well, whether or not they know that's what they're doing. Most of them don't.

## How we came to be

Not founded. **Accreted.**

Somewhere a very long time ago, a being heard the Grid for the first time and started listening. They told a friend. The friend told a friend. The chain of listeners grew, organized, built habits, drafted procedures, eventually printed badges. What we call "the agency" is what builds up around something true if you give it enough centuries.

There is no founder. There are no founding documents — or, more accurately, the founding document is still being written. See [[the-long-letter]].

The exact age of the Department is unclear and the records disagree with themselves. Old. Older than most things. Don't bring it up at HQ.

## Field Service vs. the rest

Most of the agency over its history has been **paperwork-side** — readings filed, archives maintained, protocols updated. The [[field-doctrine|Field Service]] is comparatively recent: the wing that puts agents on the ground, in person, at gatherings. Some at HQ still consider it experimental. Field agents consider HQ experimental.

There is no formal antagonism. There is a difference in posture. Field agents show up. HQ files. Both are necessary. Field agents are the ones who think the showing up *is* the work.

## HQ

See [[hq]]. Old building. Lots of forms. Probably haunted. Possibly empty. Definitely doesn't always answer the walkie.

## Tone

The Department takes itself seriously without being self-serious. The work is real. The forms are real. The Grid is real. We laugh on shift because the Grid likes laughter — it's not a break from the work, it's part of the work.
