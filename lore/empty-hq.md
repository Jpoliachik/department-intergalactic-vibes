# Is HQ Empty?

A quiet thread that runs through field-side conversations. Not a conspiracy. Not a scandal. Just a question nobody at [[the-department|HQ]] can quite answer, and nobody at HQ seems especially eager to.

## The observation

The agency runs. Badges arrive. Paychecks clear. Forms get processed. Memos get signed.

But:

- No current field agent has met someone in charge.
- Agents sent to HQ on visits come back with descriptions that don't agree with each other.
- Signatures on memos are old and reused.
- Decisions seem to happen, but it's hard to trace where.
- The walkie-talkie sometimes carries voices, but mostly carries wind.

## The possibilities

1. **HQ is staffed normally and we just don't have access.** Plausible. Boring.
2. **HQ is staffed by something not quite a person anymore.** A few agents have suggested this carefully. Nobody pushes.
3. **HQ is empty.** Has been for centuries. The agency is running on momentum, habit, and the conscientiousness of field agents. The forms process themselves. The badges arrive because they always have.
4. **HQ was never the point.** The Department's center of gravity was always the field. HQ is scaffolding. The work was always going to be done out here.

## Why it matters (and why it doesn't)

If HQ is empty, the [[resonance-grid|Grid]] still gets tended — because we're the ones tending it. The work doesn't depend on the building.

If HQ is empty, the question is who keeps mailing the badges. And nobody has a good answer to that.

## Field doctrine on the thread

Don't get worked up about it. The work is the work. If HQ is full of beings doing their jobs, great. If HQ is a thousand-year-old empty building still operating on inertia, also great. The Grid doesn't care which one. Neither, mostly, do we.

## Open questions

- Who signs the memos.
- Who mails the badges.
- What the wind in the walkie is actually wind in.
