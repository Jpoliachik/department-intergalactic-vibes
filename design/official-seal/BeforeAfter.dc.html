The balance pass
The problem was one measure: the type band was 9.7 units deep against a 79-unit centre, so the ring read as a caption wrapped around a logo rather than as half of one mark. The band is now 12.2 deep against 76 — and the rules moved in with it, because growing the type without moving the rules just crowds the type against them.
