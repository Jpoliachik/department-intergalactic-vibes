The two applications
Option B on both, with the rainbow treatment — both objects are printed in full colour, so they can carry it. These pull in opposite directions and it is worth naming: the hat is read at twenty paces on a curved surface, where shape wins and words barely register; the badge is read at arm’s length, where the words are the point. One emblem has to sit between them, which is the argument for Option B over the two extremes.
