The two applications
Option B on both, one colour on the uniform purple. The website rides the brim on the hat and the footer on the badge — outside the mark in both cases, because the emblem’s ring is full and a third line inside it would undo the balance. These two objects pull in opposite directions and it is worth naming: the hat is read at twenty paces on a curved surface, where shape wins and words barely register; the badge is read at arm’s length, where the words are the point. One emblem has to sit between them, which is the argument for Option B over the two extremes.
