Scale
The bigger type keeps buying scale, but less than the last pass suggested. Read the row rather than the claim: 64px — about 17mm — is the honest floor for the beaded one, where the beads begin closing up and CERTIFIED starts to fill in. The unbeaded one is still usable at 48px. Both fail at 36. For anything smaller the bare glyph is the answer, not a shrunk seal.
