# Department of Intergalactic Vibrations (D.I.V.)

A fictional cosmic agency Justin and friends play as live characters at festivals — debuting at **Seven Stars**. Costumes, backstory, artifacts (badges, cards, stickers, totem, gadgets), and a recruitment mechanic to draw festival-goers into the universe.

## Repository layout

- **`BRAND.md`** — Foundation: universe, tone, visual identity, interaction tiers. **Read this first** before working on anything related to D.I.V.
- **`LOGO-EXPLORATION.md`** — Wide spread of logo directions with reference points and image-generation prompts for iteration.

## Working on this project

- **Iterate decisions one at a time.** This project was built by running individual decisions past Justin before committing anything to docs. Continue that pattern — don't bulk-draft, ask first.
- **Keep the tone in mind.** Whimsical, goofy, good-vibes, with MIB-mystery flavor. Not cynical, not bureaucratic-jaded.
- **Brand sharpness.** Brand explicitly says NOT New Age, NOT crystals-and-mandalas, NOT modern startup chrome. Astrology and psychedelia appear as *operational framework* and *visual texture* respectively — not as belief systems.
- **Festival = Seven Stars = the Pleiades.** The crew this weekend is the "Pleiades Field Office" detachment.

## Key locked decisions (see BRAND.md for full detail)

- Crew = purple hi-vis utility workers maintaining the galactic Resonance Grid
- Aesthetic = cosmic industrial (trade union + retro space program) with psychedelic texture
- Interaction tiers: sticker (slap & go) → commendation card → deep recruit
- On-duty in uniform; off-duty at campsite
